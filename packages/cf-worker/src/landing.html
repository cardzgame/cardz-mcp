6 Read-Only Public Tools

  • search_cards — Exact-printing search by card name, EN/ZH/JA/KO alias, JLP standard alias (e.g. 2023 Pikachu [SV2a 025/165] JP), or public external ID (tcgplayer:*, snkrdunk:*).
  • get_card — Canonical ID or external ID lookup with PSA 10 reference/verified prices, side-by-side US/JP/CN quotes, population, market cap, and daily history.
  • get_market — Published rankings by market cap, 30d volume, sales count, or 30d change.
  • get_card_ohlcv — 2025+ daily (1d) and weekly (1w) OHLCV candles up to 730 days.
  • get_market_depth — Aggregated cross-region (US, JP, CN, CARDZ_MKT) depth bands for Top 500 cards.
  • evaluate_portfolio — Batch valuation & unrealized PnL for up to 50 cards with explicit ambiguity candidates.
